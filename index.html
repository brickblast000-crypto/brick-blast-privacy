Brick Blast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Brick Blast ("the Game") is a mobile puzzle/arcade game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Brick Blast does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, phone number, or other directly identifying information to play the Game.

2. Local Game Data

The Game may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as level progress, settings, achievements, coins, gems, and other game progress. This information is used to provide the Game's features and is not intended to identify you personally.

3. Advertising

The Game may display optional rewarded advertisements, such as an advertisement that can be watched to receive an in-game reward. If advertising services are used, third-party advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information about ad interactions in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not control the data practices of third-party advertising providers. Their own privacy policies apply to the information they process.

4. Children's Privacy

Brick Blast is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact the developer through the contact method available on the platform where the Game is distributed.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services for functions such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or distribution. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no electronic service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's features or third-party services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Brick Blast, please use the developer contact information provided on the platform where the Game is published.
